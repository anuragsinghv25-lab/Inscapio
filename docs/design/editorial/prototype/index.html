Product direction and design principles

One sentence. InScapio stays “where knowledge becomes an experience”. The new Edition adds a daily, human-curated front page whose stories are structured explainers, not articles with a skin on top. Everything shown here is a proposal; the screen inventory in 01-screen-inventory.md says what exists.

Conflict to resolve (Q-45): docs/product/positioning.md says “timeless understanding rather than timely reporting”. A daily edition is timely. Recommended resolution: keep the product promise (understand, don’t just consume) and add an “Edition” layer; each story links to a timeless explainer when one exists.

Principles

#PrincipleWhat it changes in the design
1Reading first, interaction secondEvery story is fully understandable as text. Timeline, chart and stakeholder blocks are expansions, never gates.
2Say how we knowEvery claim carries an epistemic label (Established / Attributed / Contested / Analysis / Scenario / Unresolved) shown as text + icon, never colour alone.
3A human signs every storyPublication requires an explicit, named approval. Public pages say “Reviewed and published by the editor”, and disclose AI assistance in the method note.
4Corrections are visibleUpdated vs Corrected are different states with their own banner and a public change log. Unpublished stories leave a tombstone, not a 404.
5Calm motionNothing moves unless the reader asks, except one optional headline rail that is manual by default (see Page 2).
6Premium, not “newsy”Reuse the fog / paper / deep-teal palette, Bricolage display + Literata text, flat shapes, 2–4px radii, hard edges. No red breaking-news bars, no stock-photo grids.
7Public readers never sign inNo account prompts, paywall, or cookie wall on the public Edition. Auth exists only for the publisher and admin.

Proposed information architecture

/ Edition front page (Today) [replaces ReadExploreStrip hero block] /story/[slug] Story page (kind: news) [new content type] /topics All topics (index) /topics/[topic] Topic page: current stories + explainers /explore NEW small index of existing experiences (today: only the #rooms anchor on /) /experiences/[slug] Existing experience pages (kept, unchanged) /about /method /corrections /ai-use Editorial policy pages (new copy; counsel review) /studio/* Private workspace (publisher/admin only; noindex; not linked from public nav) /sign-in /invite/[token] /mfa Auth (never required to read)

Starting taxonomy (recommended, 6 topics, not the 10+ suggested): Conflict & security · Diplomacy & institutions · Trade & economy · Energy & resources · Technology & power · India & the world. “Global economics” becomes Trade & economy so it can host economics stories later without a new top-level section. Regions are a filter, not a nav tree.

Journeys

Reader (no account)

Land on Edition→Scan lead + rail→Open story→Read brief→Explore timeline / chart→Check sources→Related story or explainer

Publisher / admin (daily, 5–10 stories)

Sign in + MFA→Queue→Import AI-drafted JSON→Fix validation errors→Edit text / sources→Preview desktop + mobile→Confirm & publish→Public URL
New info→Open story→Choose Update / Correct / Unpublish→Write public note→Confirm

Honest effort estimate: even with the Editorial MVP built, a careful story takes real review time. 5–10 per day is realistic only if import + validation are fast; this is a risk in 06-decisions-assumptions-risks.md.

      Editorial method · draft policy copy

      How we work

      Draft copy for owner review. One editor chooses stories. Drafts may be prepared with an AI tool from the sources listed on the page; the editor checks every claim and approves publication. We label what is established, attributed, contested, analysis or scenario. Updates add information; corrections fix errors; both are dated and logged. Unpublished stories keep a notice explaining why.
